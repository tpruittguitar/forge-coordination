Degree FLEX means evidence of explicit degree requirement, preference or accepted experience equivalency, not a promise that a degree will be waived. Exact links must bind to the specific live posting/requisition. Preserve original initiating/source provenance; never substitute a corporate homepage.
Make supported research judgments and state the basis. Ordinary ambiguity, unreadable sources, missing facts, return transport and usage exhaustion remain AI/CODE-owned with a retry condition. Existing human decisions, protected applications/declines, mandatory permissions and genuine personal decisions remain protected; this instruction does not silently approve or reverse them.
