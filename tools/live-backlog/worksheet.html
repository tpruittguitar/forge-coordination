Salary range shows salary recorded in the source posting. Estimates are labeled separately. Currency and pay period are shown only if recorded; an unlabeled $ is not assumed to be USD or annual pay. ATS hits counts distinct resume-supported competencies matched in saved title or role text. This is preliminary evidence, not an ATS-provider score, percentage or qualification verdict. Title-only and partial content can miss matches. Zero means no observed matches in that text, not a failed application; — means usable evidence is unavailable. Matched words lists the saved words and phrases, which can outnumber the competency count. All recorded terms are shown; title-only or partial source text can omit other matches. Evidence shows saved excerpts (which may be shortened), the resume version and check date.
