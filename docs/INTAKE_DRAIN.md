It is a work inventory, never a second job master. Disappearing source groups remain reconciliation work, not completed jobs. Original email/local/AUTO request fences survive employer URL changes.

Exact company/title/site, a positive role-content quote and source version must agree. Current function-mismatch rules, canonical Writer business policy, duplicate/protected decisions, original request IDs, receipt completion and exact readback gates remain in force. Unsupported or inaccessible sources need further research only for genuinely missing or conflicting facts; no unbound AI prose authorizes intake. Complete source-bound cards do not require a leadership title, positive scope verdict, or separate scope re-fetch before entering intake. Function ranking remains advisory. Missing identity facts and original transaction fences still require their existing recovery paths; unknown requirements or employer business do not block an otherwise identified lead.
