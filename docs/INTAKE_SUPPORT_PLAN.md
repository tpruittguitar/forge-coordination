# Discovery and intake support plan

Owner: ChatGPT/Codex. Authority: Tim's November 5, 2026 instructions in this chat, including the attached October 13 resume. This plan covers discovery evidence and the entire captured intake backlog through verified Writer outcomes. It does not change Tim's saved decisions, canonical scoring or application protections.

## Intended result

Every captured candidate has a durable identity, current owner, specific next action, evidence references, attempts/retry condition and recorded outcome. Complete independent candidates continue while other cases need research or reconciliation. Tim receives genuine decisions and access requirements, rather than routine AI work. Research preparation, submission and verified canonical completion remain distinct.

The dashboard's 5,418 are captured candidate identities, not 5,418 already eligible jobs. The baseline includes unclear function, omitted fields, duplicate/identity questions and possible earlier Writer requests. All remain accounted until an evidenced outcome exists. New arrivals are included in the same inventory.

## Discovery behavior and auditable pass reasons

1. Keep discovery lightweight: use the current title-family/level/location selection, returned ATS content and a single exact detail read when the listing lacks its content and that route is supported. No full enrichment or broad repeated employer research is added.
2. Preserve available descriptions from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and JobSpy. For selected Workday cards, inspect its exact detail endpoint within the existing run budget. Missing/blocked/unsupported details remain explicit. Source limits never become a false successful review.
3. Match observable posting/title terms to competencies supported by the user-supplied resume. Record the resume PDF hash, extracted-text/profile version, competency IDs, posting terms/excerpts and private resume-evidence hashes. This is explainable evidence overlap, not a claimed ATS vendor score or invented percentage.
4. Record `selectionReason` and `discoveryReview` for each passing candidate: source URL/provider/time, title family/level/location, matched function/responsibility terms, exact source excerpts/hash, resume support and unknowns. An identity/title-only pass must say so.
5. Carry that evidence through discovery reports, upstream inventory, waiting-list data and intake. Writer-supported SCOUT_NOTES carries a compact audit reference and reason; full evidence remains in source-bound artifacts. Retain the initiating source when a stronger employer URL is recovered.
6. Historical jobs without a recorded discovery reason get an explicit missing-rationale marker. Never fabricate a historical review or rewrite it as if performed at discovery time.

Scope review is deeper than discovery: it establishes what the role actually owns and does. It reuses discovery evidence and seeks only the remaining fact. A title-level match does not prove factory responsibility, technical depth, team size or reporting level.

## One durable drain inventory

`state/INTAKE_DRAIN.json` derives every upstream candidate group and its aliases, including historical discovery/email inventory. It records identity, source version, original discovery rationale, saved recovery evidence, task owner, action, specific next action, request fence, review/validation proof, retry state and upstream outcome. It is a work inventory, not another job master.

| Next action | Owner |
|---|---|
| REVIEW_SCOPE | FORGE_INTAKE_SCOPE_REVIEW / ChatGPT |
| RECOVER_SOURCE | FORGE_INTAKE_SOURCE_RECOVERY / ChatGPT, after saved-field recovery in code |

These actions are disjoint. Old intake/relevance helpers skip the drain-owned keys; their already returned evidence stays available and immutable. Existing enrichment, pay, mailbox and outcome assignments continue. Workers use code-selected keys and resume cursors, not independent duplicate lists. Candidate counts, source occurrences, research returns and canonical jobs are different units.

## Worker rules and handoff

Three thin shared-entry ChatGPT utilities run hourly, staggered. The scope worker starts at :02, source recovery at :22 and held reconciliation at :42 Eastern time; configuration readback must confirm activation. These are operating choices for the authorized processing request, not promises of measured throughput. The existing Writer workflow keeps its hourly :17 catch-up plus successful discovery triggers; discovery schedules stay unchanged.

Workers reuse saved full-source evidence, previous research, exact ATS details and the resume profile. Only missing/ambiguous facts require targeted AI research. Existing one-fact-changing-lookup rules remain; artifact sizes are checkpoint boundaries, not daily/WIP/processing caps. A blocked host does not stop accessible independent sources. Capture usable evidence before moving to the next candidate.

Each review has sourceVersion, genuine provider/task/run identity, observation time, exact sources/attempts, conclusion, reason and next action. SOURCE_READY includes complete observed identity, positive function/responsibility quote and current resume competency binding. The code independently re-fetches/parses supported exact postings and checks identity, quote and resume overlap before READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_INTAKE. A positive AI statement alone cannot write a job, override an active function-exclusion rule or close a Writer fence.

Missing/unreadable/conflicting sources become WAITING_SOURCE_CHANGE with a concrete retry condition. Repeated programmed source failures transfer to the source-recovery AI after two recorded attempts. The older shared AI exception list defers drain-owned candidate/request cases, retaining mailbox, enrichment and historical coverage work without duplicate research. Source validation failures expose the exact reason and a six-hour retry boundary. Old unchanged research is reused rather than repeatedly billed. Changed source identity/evidence/resume version invalidates stale research. Independent validation expires after 24 hours and must be refreshed before intake. Disappearing inventory becomes reconciliation work, never silent completion.

## Held cases and canonical protection

Held is normally automation-owned work. Code first checks original requests and independently verified outcomes. AI investigates actual employer/title/site/requisition evidence or missing original responses. The existing read-only probe workflow may inspect bound AUTO run IDs using its current GitHub secret; cloud AI never obtains that credential. Supported automatic recovery remains with the existing Writer code.

An uncertain write is never resubmitted under another request ID, posting URL or worker. Fences include saved email, local intake-plan and AUTO requests; known employer/title families stay fenced across URL changes. A plausible current row does not independently prove which historical transaction wrote it. Missing proof remains explicit while other work proceeds.

Existing applied/declined repeat rules, separate-facility safeguards, source provenance and protected states remain. Writer retains final Never Consider, canonical identity/admission, mutation, counts and scoring authority. Intake does not require missing pay/degree/client details to be perfected; unknowns carry into enrichment. Research does not send applications, recruiter outreach, email changes or calendar invitations.

## Outcomes, support and monitoring

Record prepared evidence, independently validated candidates, Writer submissions, verified new jobs, verified existing matches, supported exclusions and unresolved holds separately. Only source-bound canonical presence/exclusion and valid Writer proofs establish completion. A green workflow, uploaded artifact, prepared batch, API response or disappearing queue item is insufficient.

Report arrival rate, resolved candidates/day, verified new jobs/day, outstanding counts by action/status, oldest actionable age, source-access failures, original Writer requests and actual Tim decisions. Assess drainage as arrivals versus evidenced resolutions; do not promise a clearance date before measured rates exist. Avoid treating a missing measurement as zero completed work.

Notify Tim for meaningful progress, completion, failure requiring action or a genuine choice. Repeated unchanged waits stay quiet. Source research, routine parsing, identity investigation and receipt reconciliation stay assigned to automation. A request to Tim states the exact decision, evidence and effect on that candidate; it does not stop unrelated work.
