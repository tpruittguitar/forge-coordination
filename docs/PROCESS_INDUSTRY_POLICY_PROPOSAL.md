# Process-industry exclusion proposal

Draft only. Tim clarified that the intended exclusion is process-industry operations such as bulk chemicals, refining and raw-material production, while discrete stamping, molding and machining remain in scope. This file does not edit or activate canonical policy, assign an existing NC ID, or authorize row mutations.

Proposed addition to the canonical policy, with a stable rule/ruling ID assigned through its normal policy workflow:

SECTION=PROCESS_INDUSTRY_EXCLUSION

- Exclude employers whose primary business is bulk chemical production, refining, or raw-material production through process-industry operations. Apply consistently to discovery, manual and automated intake, enrichment, scoring, ranking, recommendations and resurfacing.
- Keep discrete manufacturing such as stamping, molding and machining in scope. Do not classify an employer as excluded merely because it processes a material, uses chemicals, performs industrial process engineering, or supplies machinery, automation, components or services to process-industry customers. Diversified employers and uncertain business boundaries require review rather than a keyword exclusion.
- Reuse sourced company-business facts already gathered. Do not add mandatory company research to discovery or block an otherwise plausible role merely because employer business is unknown. For agency postings, classify the identified client using grounded client identity; unknown clients remain review-needed and continue under the existing agency rules.
- A confirmed match must have exact employer identity, primary-business evidence, source URL and quotation, verification date, confidence, and the current rule definition/revision. Site products alone do not prove employer primary business.
- For existing active rows, use authorized Writer dispositions with row-specific receipts and independent readback. Preserve applications, rejections, explicit Tim decisions, terminal protections and audit history. Never delete rows to apply this rule.

Before activation: assign the stable identifier, bind reviewed examples to the approved definition/revision, review raw-material boundary cases individually, and run the full-population dry run. This proposal does not introduce exclusions for stamping, molding or machining.
