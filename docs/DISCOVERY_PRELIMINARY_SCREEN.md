# Preliminary discovery screening

Discovery records a preliminary resume overlap and current Never Consider review before canonical intake. The current grounded profile is `control/RESUME_MATCH_PROFILE.json`, tied to Tim's October 13 resume attachment, its text/PDF hashes and supported competency evidence. The older September approved-resume setting must not replace this newer user-provided resume. Each production screen context reads current Writer rules and resume-folder metadata once. A newer resume or a modified matching resume requires profile refresh; old overlap is not presented as current.

Automatic board/source discovery and initial original-alert capture share the screen. Email title-only cards remain provisional. The observability publisher also screens saved captured evidence before publishing waiting queues, and AUTO intake rechecks against current rules before selection. Read failures retain the candidate with explicit unknowns; low keyword overlap never rejects it. No final ATS percentage, fit score, degree equivalency or qualification is invented.

A Never Consider exclusion requires an ACTIVE canonical rule, an exact supported definition, source URL, and an explicit employer-business statement captured in posting content. Job title, employer name, required industry experience and unnamed agency clients cannot exclude. Supplier/equipment/software/engineering-service exceptions take precedence. Changed definitions remain interpretation work. Rule hash, rule ID, exact quote, content hash and source URL remain attached to the captured candidate. Exclusions do not delete records or mutate application/decline states. A fresh rule check can reopen source screening; it cannot reopen canonical protected jobs or clear uncertain Writer transactions.

Discovery throughput counts captured candidate identities at first observation, including candidates later excluded, without requiring a master ID. Existing known aliases are merged; unresolved cross-source duplicates remain explicitly candidate identities. WIP is accumulated stock, not a numerator for the current hour window. The app displays candidates/hour with one decimal and Unknown for missing measurements. Intake and enrichment retain jobs/hour.
