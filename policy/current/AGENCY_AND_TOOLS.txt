AGENCY AND SOURCE RULES
Agency rows and client rows, and different agencies' rows, are distinct opportunities: never merge, mark DUP_OF, or rename an existing agency COMPANY. At most two client searches: exact title + city + pay; exact title + distinctive phrase. Unknown client: COMPANY=<agency>, EMPLOYER_PRIMARY_BUSINESS=RECRUITING_AGENCY, SCOUT_NOTES="AGENCY_POSTING; CLIENT=UNKNOWN; CLIENT_SEARCH=BASIC_DONE", EMPLOYER_DOMAIN_HINT=<stated client industry>. A client learned later goes in CLIENT_EMPLOYER, CLIENT_EMPLOYER_EVIDENCE_URL and CLIENT_EMPLOYER_ASOF. Agency liveness comes from the agency's own page, not the client's posting. Existing ambiguity between CONTROL agency items 1 and 3 for a client identified BEFORE initial intake remains unresolved: preserve the source and hold that identity decision for Tim; do not choose a new identity convention. This does not block unknown-client intake or unrelated work.
TinyFish fetch_content/search may be used when available: up to 10 URLs, ttl 0, per_url_timeout_ms 60000; Workday discovery links=true, include_selectors=["#mainContent"]. Never use metered agent/browser/monitor tools without Tim, sign in, submit forms, apply or attempt CAPTCHA. Missing connector: TINYFISH_UNAVAILABLE and use an otherwise permitted method. Verify current capabilities; old test anecdotes are not proof of runtime availability.
LIVENESS: LIVE_EMPLOYER_ATS when the first-party posting is open; LIVE_SECONDARY for a dated secondary posting within 30 days (record page date); CLOSED only on positive closure/removal evidence; otherwise UNCONFIRMED with what was tried. A block/login/403/429/network error is never closure. Posting age alone never closes a first-party job. Do not re-stamp liveness already resolved by ATS_CHECK_RESULT=LIVE/CLOSED. Workday missing-page evidence requires a requisition-bearing URL; without its token, find the exact job link first. A LinkedIn "No longer accepting applications" closes only a row whose sole source is that LinkedIn posting.
