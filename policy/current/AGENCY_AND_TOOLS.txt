AGENCY AND SOURCE RULES
Agency rows and client rows, and different agencies' rows, are distinct opportunities: never merge, mark DUP_OF, or rename an existing agency COMPANY. At most two client searches: exact title + city + pay; exact title + distinctive phrase. Unknown client: COMPANY=<agency>, EMPLOYER_PRIMARY_BUSINESS=RECRUITING_AGENCY, SCOUT_NOTES="AGENCY_POSTING; CLIENT=UNKNOWN; CLIENT_SEARCH=BASIC_DONE", EMPLOYER_DOMAIN_HINT=<stated client industry>. A client learned later goes in CLIENT_EMPLOYER, CLIENT_EMPLOYER_EVIDENCE_URL and CLIENT_EMPLOYER_ASOF. Client known at intake (named in the posting, or found by the two searches with matching title and location): submit the row under the client as an ordinary employer posting with the agency URL as SOURCE_URL (Agency amendment 2026-10-13 item 1; Tim's ruling on known-client identity is open as forge-coordination asks/open/ASK-20261018-11.json; keep doing item 1 until it is answered). No further client research on an agency row unless Tim asks or the row reaches interview. Scoring and strategic/feasibility analysis may use CLIENT_EMPLOYER when present; without it, score from the posting text and record company-dependent fields UNKNOWN. A repeat sighting of the same agency posting still dedupes normally. Agency liveness (Tim: "Close it if the recruiter closes it"): the agency's own job page or ATS is that row's first-party source. Open there = LIVE_EMPLOYER_ATS with LIVENESS_NOTE "agency page". A 404/410, a closed or filled flag, or removal from the agency's own board = CLOSED (positive evidence; LIVENESS_METHOD names the agency page). A dated job-board copy within 30 days = LIVE_SECONDARY. The client's posting never opens or closes the agency row, and the agency row never opens or closes the client row.
TinyFish fetch_content/search may be used when available: up to 10 URLs, ttl 0, per_url_timeout_ms 60000; Workday discovery links=true, include_selectors=["#mainContent"]. Never use metered agent/browser/monitor tools without Tim, sign in, submit forms, apply or attempt CAPTCHA. Missing connector: TINYFISH_UNAVAILABLE and use an otherwise permitted method. Verify current capabilities; old test anecdotes are not proof of runtime availability.
LIVENESS: LIVE_EMPLOYER_ATS when the first-party posting is open; LIVE_SECONDARY for a dated secondary posting within 30 days (record page date); CLOSED only on positive closure/removal evidence; otherwise UNCONFIRMED with what was tried. A block/login/403/429/network error is never closure. Posting age alone never closes a first-party job. Do not re-stamp liveness already resolved by ATS_CHECK_RESULT=LIVE/CLOSED. Workday missing-page evidence requires a requisition-bearing URL; without its token, find the exact job link first. A LinkedIn "No longer accepting applications" closes only a row whose sole source is that LinkedIn posting.
