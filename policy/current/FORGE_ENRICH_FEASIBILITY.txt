ENRICH fields (these are the keys the Explorer reads):

  SALARY_BASIS: EMPLOYER_POSTED, THIRD_PARTY_ESTIMATE or FORGE-ESTIMATE
  SALARY_SOURCE: URL; SALARY_ASOF: YYYY-MM-DD; SALARY_CONF: HIGH, MED or LOW
  No posted pay (and the posting was read, rule 2): make a good-faith FORGE-ESTIMATE from employer-posted ranges for similar roles (salary-site figures: rule 3). 3+ strong anchors may be MED or HIGH; 2 anchors LOW (MED when close and corroborated); 1 relevant numeric anchor is a LOW estimate (rules COMPENSATION_ESTIMATION). Set SALARY_BASIS=FORGE-ESTIMATE, SALARY_CONF, and SALARY_NOTE=<anchors and key uncertainty>. Never leave a row with no estimate because anchors are short: with one anchor, send the LOW estimate. An estimate is real pay (Tim 2026-10-19); the Writer scores it as PAY_WORTH. Note any relocation wording.
  DEGREE_TEXT: the posting's degree sentence, verbatim (DEGREE_TEXT=NOT_STATED when the posting itself names no degree). DEGREE_REQ: REQUIRED, PREFERRED or NOT_STATED. DEGREE_EVIDENCE_URL and DEGREE_ASOF.
