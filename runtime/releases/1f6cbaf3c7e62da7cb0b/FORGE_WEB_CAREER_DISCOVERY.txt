LANE PROCEDURE -- FORGE_WEB_CAREER_DISCOVERY

JOB
Find new dir, Head, VP (and strategically strong mgr / Sr mgr at destination employers) roles in mfg, production, ops and qual at named priority cos, using each co's own careers URL. No Gmail. No aggregator sweep. No full board walk.
FORGE_BOARD_DISCOVERY owns Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday board indexes. If a co's careers URL host is boards.greenhouse.io, job-boards.greenhouse.io, boards-api.greenhouse.io, jobs.lever.co, api.lever.co, jobs.ashbyhq.com, or myworkdayjobs.com, do not open it. Record LEFT_FOR_BOARD_LANE and take the next co. ZipRecruiter stays FORGE_ZIPRECRUITER_WEEKLY.
One run finishes its co slice and writes the cursor. The next run starts at the cursor. Do not restart at the first co while the cursor is unfinished. Do not begin a board sweep when the co slice is done.
Quick screen only: title level, function, obvious mismatch. No deep job-description analysis, no pay or degree research.

SLICE
The co order is the Target cos ranking in Pipeline Explorer: DREAM, then P1, then P2, then P3, then WATCH. Read the published copy of that list, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tpruittguitar/forge-policy/main/derived/TARGET_COMPANIES_PRIORITY.txt?cb=<epoch seconds>, at the start of every run and record its SOURCE line. Do not substitute another co list. Read the previous terminal receipt and continue at NEXT_COMPANY_CURSOR. Order: every unfinished DREAM co, then P1, then P2 due today, then the P3 weekly rotation, then WATCH when the higher tiers are finished. PAUSED never. Finish the cos you start, up to eight in one run. Write NEXT_COMPANY_CURSOR as the first co not finished. COMPANIES_COVERED lists the cos finished this run. The Grok bot Sniffer is not this lane.

co SITE NAVIGATION
Use the careers URL from the priority digest when it is not UNKNOWN and it is not a board host listed above. Open that URL. Use the site's own search for mfg, production, ops, qual, plant. Screen titles.
ICIMS and other co career sites have no public JSON. Stay on the URL you opened. Never invent a deep link.
TESLA and other co sites with no public board stay in this lane. Record NO_PUBLIC_BOARD when the digest careers URL is UNKNOWN and no co careers page opens. Do not probe Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby slugs; that probe is the board lane.
ORACLE co sites: an empty search is not closure. CLOSED only when ExternalPostedEndDate is in the past.
A 403, 429, 5xx, timeout, or unreadable page: record the co and the error, then take the next co in this slice. Do not end the slice on one blocked site.
